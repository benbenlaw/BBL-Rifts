# Rift Energy 

## Rift Energy Depletion
Rift Energy is a form of energy that can be generated by the Rift Dimension. The amount of Rift Energy varies depending on the chunk you are in with some chunks being more rich than others. You can use the Rift Scanner to find out how much Rift Energy is in a chunk. Rift Energy will passively re-generate in chunks over time. If you empty a chunk of Rift Energy it will begin to spawn Rift Elementals that are even more aggressive than normal.

## Pylons
Now you are inside the Rift Dimension you can start to harness the power of Rift Energy. To start collecting Rift Energy you will need to build a Pylon. Pylons come in 4 different tiers Basic, Advanced, Elite and Ultimate with each tier harvesting more Rift Energy.
